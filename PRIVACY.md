## Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 11) you can, among other things, ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, and learn who it was shared with. Email **omerbilir.office@gmail.com**. Requests are answered free of charge and within 30 days at the latest (KVKK Article 13).

# Privacy Notice — MERLIR CHESS Volunteer Interest List

**Version 0.1.** This notice may be updated after legal review; changes will be published here.

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you join the MERLIR CHESS volunteer interest list. It is provided to inform you; it is **not** a request for consent.

## 1. Data controller

**Ömer Bilir**, a natural person and the founder of the independent MERLIR CHESS project.
Contact: **omerbilir.office@gmail.com** · GitHub: [@omerbiliroffice-jpg](https://github.com/omerbiliroffice-jpg)

MERLIR STUDIO and MERLIR GAMES are project brand names, not legal entities, and are not the data controller.

## 2. What data is processed

- Your GitHub username and the public profile information GitHub shows with it
- What you write in the interest form: areas of interest and, optionally, links or a short note about your experience
- Your confirmation that you are 18 or older
- If you email us: your email address and message

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address, ID, or any sensitive data (health, religion, political opinion, etc.). Please do not include them.

## 3. Why

Only to understand which skills are interested in the project and to contact you about possible future volunteer contributions. Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for automated decisions.

## 4. Legal basis

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Article 5(2)), the intended bases are:
- **Data you made public yourself** (Art. 5(2)(d)) — you publish the issue yourself on GitHub; we only process it for the purpose for which you published it.
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 5(2)(f)) — evaluating volunteer interest, without harming your fundamental rights and freedoms.

## 5. How data is collected

Directly from you, through a public GitHub Issue Form in this repository, or by email if you choose to write.

## 6. Who can see it and where it is stored

- **Everyone.** Issues in a public repository are visible to anyone on the internet and can be copied, indexed or archived by third parties. Deleting the issue later cannot remove those copies.
- **GitHub, Inc.** hosts the repository. GitHub stores and processes data in several countries, including the United States ([GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
- **Google** hosts the contact email account, if you email us.
- We do **not** copy your data into other systems (no spreadsheets, CRMs or mailing lists).

How Turkish rules on international data transfer (KVKK Article 9) apply to this setup is under legal review.

## 7. How long

Interest issues are kept while the interest list is open and are deleted **no later than 12 months** after they are created. Emails are deleted on the same schedule unless a contribution relationship starts.

## 8. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose, learn to whom it is transferred in Turkey or abroad, request correction, request deletion, request that third parties be notified of corrections or deletions, object to results produced solely by automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

**How to ask:** email **omerbilir.office@gmail.com**, or comment "please delete" on your own issue. Requests are answered free of charge as soon as possible and **within 30 days at the latest** (KVKK Art. 13). You can also edit or close your own issue at any time.

If you are in another country, your local data protection law may give you additional rights.

## 9. Security

The repository is managed by a single account with two-factor authentication. Only the controller can delete issues. No data from the interest list is shared with anyone else.

## 10. Age

The interest list is for people **18 or older**. If you are under 18, please do not open an issue; you can watch or star the repository instead.

## 11. Changes

Changes to this notice will be published in this repository with a new version number and date.
